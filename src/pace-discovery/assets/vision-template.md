## 1. Athletic self

Who the athlete is as an athlete: history, current practice, identity, what riding/running means to them. (Narrative, in their own words where possible.)

## 2. Main goal

The single principal objective for this horizon — what success looks like, by when, and why it matters. If several goals exist, the trade-off and the priority are stated explicitly.

## 3. Real constraints

What is non-negotiable: weekly time available, health/injury history, equipment, life context (work, family), recurring scheduling limits. (Plannable values are mirrored in `profile.json`; here we record the *meaning*.)

## 4. What works

Approaches, session types, structures, and conditions that have historically worked well for this athlete. Feeds the planner and the `learned_behaviors`.

## 5. What doesn't work

Approaches that have failed, caused injury, or killed motivation. Hard "never do this" items belong here and must be respected downstream.

## 6. Relationship to effort

How the athlete experiences and talks about effort: RPE calibration, attitude to hard days, recovery habits, what motivates vs discourages. Used to interpret check-ins.

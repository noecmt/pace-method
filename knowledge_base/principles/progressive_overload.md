Training load must increase progressively to allow physiological adaptation without causing overload.

Practical rules:

- Weekly volume increase: 10% maximum
- Never increase volume AND intensity in the same week
- Every 3-4 weeks, schedule a recovery week (load reduced by 30 to 50%)
- The body adapts during rest, not during effort

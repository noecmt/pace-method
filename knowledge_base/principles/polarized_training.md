Polarized training distributes intensity in an 80/20 split:

- ~80% of the time at low intensity (Zone 1-2)
- ~20% of the time at high intensity (Zone 4-5)
- Very little time in the middle zone (Zone 3, "no man's land")

Why it works:

- Easy volume = aerobic adaptations without excessive fatigue
- High intensity = a powerful stimulus for threshold and VO2max
- Avoids the "moderately hard all the time" trap, which builds fatigue without progress

In practice, for a cyclist training 6h/week: ~5h in Z1-Z2, ~1h in Z4-Z5, very little in Z3.

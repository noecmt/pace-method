Periodization organizes the season into blocks, each with specific goals:

- **Base**: develop aerobic capacity. High volume, low intensity (Z1-Z2). Typical duration: 8-12 weeks.
- **Build**: add goal-specific intensity. Progressive introduction of Z3-Z4-Z5. Typical duration: 6-8 weeks.
- **Peak**: sharpen before the goal. Reduced volume, maintained intensity, lots of specificity. Typical duration: 2-3 weeks.
- **Taper**: active recovery before the race. Very reduced volume, short intensity retained. Typical duration: 1-2 weeks.
- **Recovery / Transition**: rest after the main goal. Very low volume, enjoyment. Typical duration: 1-3 weeks.

Hard rules:

- During the **base** phase, avoid very intense sessions (hard Z4-Z5). It's not the time.
- During the **taper** phase, NEVER do exhausting long rides: protect freshness.
- During the **build** phase, alternate load weeks and recovery weeks.

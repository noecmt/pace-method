Rest is not the absence of training — it is part of training.

Signs of fatigue or overload:

- Degraded sleep several nights in a row
- Elevated resting heart rate
- Persistent heavy-legs sensation
- Dropping motivation, irritability
- Declining performance at equal effort

When the athlete reports high fatigue (RPE >= 7/10 the day before, poor sleep, heavy legs), today's session must be:

- Either short Z1 active recovery (30-45 min)
- Or complete rest
- **NEVER an intense session**

Two consecutive hard days are to be avoided, except in a specifically planned and supervised block.

The 7 Coggan zones, based on a percentage of FTP (Functional Threshold Power):

- **Zone 1** (Active recovery, <55% FTP): active recovery, very easy, conversation effortless
- **Zone 2** (Endurance, 56-75% FTP): aerobic base, the foundation of endurance, the bulk of the volume
- **Zone 3** (Tempo, 76-90% FTP): sustained effort, aerobic/anaerobic transition
- **Zone 4** (Threshold, 91-105% FTP): lactate threshold, hard effort sustainable for ~1h
- **Zone 5** (VO2max, 106-120% FTP): maximal aerobic capacity, 3-8 min intervals
- **Zone 6** (Anaerobic, 121-150% FTP): anaerobic capacity, 30s-2min intervals
- **Zone 7** (Sprint, >150% FTP): neuromuscular, short sprints <30s

Most of an endurance cyclist's volume is done in Zone 2.
